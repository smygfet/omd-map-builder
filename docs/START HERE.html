OMD Map Builder

Draw your own Orcs Must Die! Deathtrap maps, play them in the game, and fight a friend in versus mode. Four maps and a box of new traps are ready to go.

Install

Close the game. Double-click Install.bat in this folder. It finds the game in your Steam libraries.

Build

Open OMD Map Builder from the desktop and press Build & install. Takes a minute or two.

Play

Start the game. The maps are in the mission choice; versus maps are called VS: …

Windows may say the app is from an unknown publisher: click More info, then Run anyway.

Four maps to start with

Open any of them in the Map Maker to see how they're made, or to change them.

Siege

Helm's Deep

A castle in the mountains with three rings of walls, a round tower, the Deep, death ravines by the main gate and walkways along the walls. Orcs split between the main gate and the Deep.

Siege

B- Day Orcmaha beach

Storm the beach: orcs wade ashore from three piers, climb the bluff ramps, cross a 3 m trench and push for the rift. Bunkers with windows over the sea.

Versus

VS Parking

Two parking garages face each other across a ravine. Orcs start on the ground floor and drive up the ramps to the crystal on the open top deck. You see everything the other player builds.

Versus

VS Twin Keeps

Two mirrored keeps. The orcs come out of a camp, take a long stair corridor that's perfect for wall traps, and climb to the rift on the keep top.

Mods included

Switch each one on or off on the app's Mods page.

  • Guardian ArcherAn elf archer from OMD3 as a trap. Sell it like any trap by aiming at it.
  • Trap BarricadeA 3 m barricade with trap spots on top and all four sides.
  • Giant Flip TrapThe huge 2×2 flip trap. Throws big enemies too; how far is set on the Mods page.
  • Endless modeThe game's own missions keep going, getting harder every wave. Settings on the Mods page.
  • VS refereeTwo players, two rifts. Send enemies to your opponent with F1–F6.

For your AI

Want to make maps by describing them, or change the tool itself? Point your AI assistant at the folder For your AI:

  • AI guide - making maps.md: how maps work and how to make or change them from your notes.
  • AI guide - how it all works.md: the app, the builder and the mods, for building it further.
  • source: all the code, the map generators and the mod scripts.

Tip: draw notes right on a map with the Map Maker's Sketch tools and save. Your AI can read them from the map file.

Making your own map

  • In the app, press Open Map Maker, draw, and press Save. It goes straight into the app.
  • Floors, walls, ramps and stairs, decks, roofs, water, props, round walls: hover over a tool for help.
  • Each map needs a rift, a player start and at least one orc spawn. Orcs need straight ramps, not curved stairs.

Removing it

Close the game and double-click Uninstall.bat. The game goes back to normal; your own maps are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OMD Map Builder\maps.

After a game update, if something acts strange: in the app's Game page, remove the custom maps and switch mods off until there's a new version.

Playing together

Both players need the same maps and mods. Share maps from the app's Share page. In versus, the host is side A, the friend side B.

Known issues

  • Selling the Guardian Archer gives no money back yet.
  • The mods run on the host's game.
